Brief Biography

I am a dynamic, result-oriented graduate of agricultural science with about six years of experience in agribusiness, agrotechnology, research and crop production. I hold an MSc in Crop Science (Cum Laude) from the University of KwaZulu-Natal and an MBA, and I am a Mandela Rhodes Foundation alumnus.

I am the Co-Founder & CEO of Farm4us, a venture that brings dormant farmland back into production, and I am passionate about sustainable agriculture, the circular nutrient economy and innovation that empowers communities.
