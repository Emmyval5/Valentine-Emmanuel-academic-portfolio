About Me

I grew up in Opulega, a farming community in Nigeria, where I saw how outdated practices lead to poor yields and food insecurity. That experience drives my work. In 2019 I became the first graduate of my department at Kogi State University with First Class Honours in Crop Science since it began in 1999, and in 2025 I was awarded the Mandela Rhodes Scholarship.

Career Aspirations
