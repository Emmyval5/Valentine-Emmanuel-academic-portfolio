My startup

Farm4us

Reviving dormant farmland and strengthening food security in rural Nigeria, one farmer and one hectare at a time.

Visit farm4us.com

The Problem

More than 25 million Nigerians face food shortages, while large areas of fertile land in rural communities lie unused. Many smallholder farmers lack the support, modern practices and market access they need to raise their yields.

Our Solution

Farm4us is an agribusiness platform that leases dormant arable land from rural landowners and puts it to work for sustainable crop production. It was inspired by my time at Babban Gona, an agritech company supporting smallholder farmers in northern Nigeria.

  • Demonstration farms that show better practices
  • Agricultural extension and farmer support
  • Modern, sustainable farming methods
  • Improved access to markets
  • Jobs and opportunities for young people
